## The poem

> A still pond. I asked it nothing.
> It gave me back my strength, clearer.
> To ask is a door; to give, the threshold.
> I gave — and came home to myself.

A still pond, a silent asker, and a giver who sees his own strength
reflected back, clearer. The last line turns: giving returns the giver
to himself. Written in the manner of Rilke's quiet, attentive images
and Shakespeare's dense paradox and closing volta.
